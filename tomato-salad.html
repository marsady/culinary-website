Tomato & Cheese Salad

Ingredients:

  • 3 tomatoes
  • 200g mozzarella or feta cheese
  • 1 red onion
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Olive oil
  • Balsamic vinegar
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Slice the tomatoes and arrange them on a serving plate.
  2. Slice the mozzarella or feta cheese and add it on top of the tomatoes.
  3. Thinly slice the red onion and sprinkle over the salad.
  4. Add fresh basil leaves on top of the plate.
  5. Add olive oil and balsamic vinegar.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste, and serve fresh!
